Privacy Policy

ClearViewAI

Last Updated

30 September 2026

Overview

ClearViewAI is designed to help users capture, recognise, read, enhance and translate text using optical character recognition (OCR) and artificial intelligence.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClearViewAI collects or processes, why it is needed, how it is used, the external services involved, and the choices available to you.

ClearViewAI does not sell your personal information and does not use images or recognised text submitted through the app for advertising.

Information We Collect and Process

Depending on the features you choose to use, ClearViewAI may collect or process the following information:

  • Your email address and account information.
  • Your user or account identifier.
  • Authentication and session information.
  • Basic Google account information if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Images captured using your device camera.
  • Images you choose to submit for processing.
  • Text recognised from images using OCR.
  • Text submitted for translation or AI-assisted processing.
  • Subscription status and purchase history.
  • Limited technical information required to operate, secure and maintain the app and its services.

Camera Access

ClearViewAI may request permission to access your device's camera. Camera access is used only when you choose to capture an image for a feature that requires it, such as scanning or recognising text.

ClearViewAI does not continuously access your camera or capture images without your action.

You can change or revoke camera permission at any time through your iPhone or iPad settings. Features that rely on the camera may stop working if camera permission is disabled.

Images and OCR Processing

Images submitted through ClearViewAI may be securely transmitted to external processing services in order to recognise and extract text, enhance image quality or repair recognised text.

ClearViewAI does not maintain a permanent library or database of the images you submit solely for OCR or AI processing.

Images and recognised text may temporarily pass through ClearViewAI's backend infrastructure and external service providers as necessary to perform the feature you requested.

Google Cloud Vision

ClearViewAI uses Google Cloud Vision API to provide optical character recognition (OCR) and extract text from images.

Images are sent to Google Cloud Vision only where necessary to perform the OCR functionality requested by the user.

According to Google's documentation, image data submitted using immediate-response Cloud Vision operations is processed in memory and is not persisted to disk.

AI Image Enhancement and Text Repair

ClearViewAI uses Replicate to provide certain artificial intelligence-powered features.

These features may include image enhancement using Real-ESRGAN and AI-assisted OCR text repair using LLaVA 13B.

Images, text or other information required to perform these features may be transmitted to Replicate for processing.

For predictions created through Replicate's API, Replicate states that prediction inputs and outputs, including associated files, are automatically deleted after approximately one hour.

Translation

ClearViewAI uses Google Cloud Translation API to provide translation functionality.

When you choose to translate recognised or submitted text, the text required for that translation may be transmitted to Google Cloud Translation in order to provide the requested result.

ClearViewAI does not maintain a permanent database of text submitted solely for translation unless the user explicitly chooses a feature that saves that information.

Accounts and Authentication

ClearViewAI uses Supabase to provide account creation, authentication, database functionality and session management.

When you create an account, information such as your email address, account identifier, authentication information and session information may be stored in order to create and maintain your ClearViewAI account.

Supabase may also store information required by ClearViewAI to determine your account or subscription tier.

Continue with Google

ClearViewAI offers Google OAuth as an optional method of signing in.

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google authenticates your identity and ClearViewAI receives the account information authorised through the Google sign-in process, such as your email address and account identifier.

Your Google password is not provided to ClearViewAI.

Speech and Text-to-Speech

ClearViewAI may provide functionality that allows recognised text to be read aloud.

Text-to-speech functionality may use speech services available through your device or operating system. Processing performed by these services is subject to the applicable provider's privacy practices.

Subscriptions and Payments

ClearViewAI may offer subscriptions or paid features through Apple's in-app purchase system.

Payments are processed through the Apple App Store. ClearViewAI does not directly receive or store your full debit card, credit card or banking details.

ClearViewAI uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, verify purchases and determine which subscription features a user is entitled to access.

RevenueCat may process information including an app user identifier, purchase history, transaction information, subscription status and information required to validate your App Store purchase.

Subscription and payment records may also be retained by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy and legal obligations.

Backend Hosting

ClearViewAI uses Render to host its backend API and server-side functionality.

Information transmitted between ClearViewAI and the external services required to provide its features may pass through Render's infrastructure.

Expo

ClearViewAI is built using Expo, which provides mobile application development infrastructure and native services.

Where Expo services are used, limited technical information may be processed as necessary to provide those services and operate the app.

External Service Providers

ClearViewAI currently uses the following external service providers:

  • Supabase – account authentication, database services, sessions and subscription-tier information.
  • Google Cloud Vision API – optical character recognition and text extraction.
  • Google Cloud Translation API – translation functionality.
  • Google OAuth – optional Google account authentication.
  • Replicate – AI image enhancement and OCR text repair.
  • Render – backend API hosting and infrastructure.
  • Expo – mobile application framework and related services.
  • RevenueCat – subscription, purchase verification and entitlement management.
  • Apple App Store – app distribution, in-app subscriptions and payment processing.
  • Apple device services – operating system and device functionality where applicable.

How We Use Information

Information collected or processed through ClearViewAI is used only where necessary to:

  • Provide OCR and text-recognition functionality.
  • Enhance images.
  • Repair or improve OCR-generated text.
  • Translate text when requested.
  • Provide text-to-speech functionality.
  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Maintain user sessions.
  • Operate and secure the app and backend services.
  • Manage subscriptions and paid features.
  • Verify App Store purchases.
  • Determine which features your subscription provides access to.
  • Respond to support, privacy or account requests.

Data We Do Not Sell

ClearViewAI does not sell your personal information.

ClearViewAI does not sell images, recognised text, translation content or AI-processing content to advertisers or data brokers.

Advertising and Tracking

ClearViewAI does not currently use your images, recognised text or account information for behavioural advertising.

ClearViewAI does not currently use this information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Images and text submitted solely for OCR, image enhancement, translation or AI processing are not intentionally retained permanently by ClearViewAI after they are no longer required to provide the requested feature.

Some external processing providers may retain submitted information temporarily while processing it or for a limited period in accordance with their own service terms and privacy policies.

Account information, authentication information, session records, subscription-tier information and other information required to maintain your ClearViewAI account may be stored for as long as your account remains active.

Certain records may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, maintain security or keep required transaction records.

Account Deletion

ClearViewAI allows users to initiate deletion of their account directly from within the app.

When you request account deletion, your ClearViewAI account and personal information associated with that account will be deleted or anonymised where appropriate, except for information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record or similar legitimate purposes.

Deleting your ClearViewAI account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription. App Store subscriptions are managed through your Apple account and can be viewed or cancelled through Apple's subscription settings.

Information independently held by Apple or other external service providers may remain subject to those providers' own retention and deletion policies.

Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information associated with your account, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of that information.

You can delete your ClearViewAI account through the account settings provided within the app.

You may also contact us using the email address below if you have a privacy question or request relating to your personal information.

Device permissions, including camera access, can be managed through your device settings.

Security

ClearViewAI uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information transmitted and processed through the service.

Data transmitted between the app, backend and external services is handled using the security mechanisms provided by those platforms and services.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

ClearViewAI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental or guardian consent would be legally required.

Users should avoid submitting images containing personal or sensitive information about another person unless they have an appropriate reason and authority to do so.

Third-Party Services

External service providers used by ClearViewAI operate under their own privacy policies and data-processing terms.

ClearViewAI uses these providers only where reasonably necessary to provide app functionality, accounts, AI processing, infrastructure or subscriptions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ClearViewAI's features, service providers or data-handling practices change.

If the policy is updated, the revised version and its updated date will be published on this page.
